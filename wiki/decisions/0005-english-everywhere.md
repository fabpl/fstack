# Plugin and wiki in English

## Context
The author works in French. Skill instructions adhere better in English, and a wiki in one fixed language is searchable by agents regardless of the conversation language.

## Decision
Skills, agents, wiki pages and run files are written in English. f-chief answers the user in the user's language. The user's brief is kept verbatim in its original language.

## Consequences
Pages are consistent across projects. French requests are translated into English when they become specs, tickets or wiki pages.
